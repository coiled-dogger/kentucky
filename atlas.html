08 / Embedded content

Embedded and external content

<iframe> embeds another browsing context. This sandboxed example uses a simple inline document.

<object> and <embed> can host external resources, but formats and browser support vary. They are not loaded here to avoid unsafe or unreliable third-party content.

09 / Other elements

Metadata, scripting and special cases

  • <html> , <head> , <title> , <meta> , <link> , <style> , <script> , <noscript> : used in the document head and setup.
  • <body> , <main> , <header> , <footer> , <nav> , <section> , <article> , <aside> , <address> : semantic layout.
  • <map> and <area> : create clickable regions on an image; omitted from the visual demo because a useful example needs a coordinated image map.
  • <track> : supplies timed text for media; shown inside the video element above.
  • <slot> : defines a placeholder in a Web Component shadow tree and only has its intended effect in that context.
  • <template> : inert HTML that JavaScript can clone; demonstrated above.

Character references: & < > " ' and non-breaking space.
